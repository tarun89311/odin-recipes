Paradise Biryani

Paradise biryani is delicious and famous biryani in Hyderabad

Ingredients

  • 3 ½ cups water
  • 2 ⅓ cups basmati rice
  • 4 bay leaves, divided
  • one cup warm milk
  • 1 pinch saffron threads
  • one-forth cup ghee(clarified butter)
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 green chile peppers, chopped
